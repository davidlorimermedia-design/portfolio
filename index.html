SENIOR PRODUCT DESIGNER · WEIR

Industrial Apps, Field Tools & Design Systems.

Over the past two years as a Senior Product Designer at The Weir Group, I have helped evolve a lower-maturity design function and fragmented processes into a scalable digital ecosystem—growing our rustic design system into 85+ components across web, native, and industrial use cases while exploring MCP optimization. Across Weir’s excavation and manufacturing operations, I have led design for Asset Intelligence (offline field inspection of Ground Engagement Tools), the ESCO Resource Centre for dealers, and the global Daily Layered Accountability action tracker across LATAM, APAC, NAM, and EMEA.

01 · WHAT I DO

From complex problems to product direction.

Joining a lower-maturity team with fragmented processes and a rustic design system two years ago, my work starts before an interface exists—researching shop floors, mine sites, and dealer workflows to establish repeatable design foundations and clear product direction.

03 · SYSTEMS THINKING
“What capability could we create once and reuse across Weir?”

Working across multiple programmes lets me spot connections between teams and products. Instead of repeatedly solving the same interface problem, I look for shared patterns, components and capabilities that can become part of the wider digital ecosystem.
